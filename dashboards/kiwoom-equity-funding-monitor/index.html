Margin ratio & top-up

The register measures drawdown against collateral after haircut. Policy ratio is 50%; a ratio above 100% means post-haircut collateral no longer covers the outstanding.

MgRt = Used ÷ HC Value × 100
HC Value = Σ ( market value × (1 − haircut) )
Top-up(50%) = Used − 50% × HC Value

Performing ≤ 50%   Top-up 50–100%   Critical > 100% or drawn > plafond

REPO coverage

For securities financing under GMRA 2000 (ID), the exit test is collateral coverage of the second leg.

Coverage = collateral MV ÷ outstanding

Termination barrier < 150% · minimum coverage policy 200%. The live ENRG deal (acct 3-8165, Rp 25 bn) covers 577% per the Risk Management Monitor.

Fix limit vs Rainbow

Fix limit — granted once at cash + post-haircut collateral valuation; fixed thereafter, any stock, no per-trade haircut.

Fix = cash + Σ MV × (1 − HC)

Rainbow — recomputed live; higher-haircut purchases carry a higher rate and a smaller limit.

Rainbow = (cash − BOO + unsettled + value after HC) × (1 − fee) ÷ rate

What the Board should read from this

Who — nine Group PICs act as liquidity providers on named stock projects; each accordion card is one PIC's book.

How big — total plafond Rp 160,3 bn against MKBD Rp 351,77 bn; the largest single group (Arief · ENRG/BNBR) holds 43,7% of all facilities.

The risk — where the margin ratio exceeds 100%, liquidating collateral at register haircuts would not repay the drawdown: that residual is potential bad debt if the stock project fails.
